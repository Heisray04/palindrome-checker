Is it a Palindrome?

A palindrome is a word or phrase that can be read the same way forwards and backwards, ignoring punctuation, case, and spacing.
NOTE: Only the letters a to z and the digits 0 to 9 are checked. Signs and symbols can be typed, but they have no effect on the result. The characters ">" and "<" are not supported.
